Common questions

How do I restore a Pro purchase?
Open HabitGorilla → Settings → Restore Purchase. This re-links your existing subscription to the app without charging you again.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → HabitGorilla Pro. Apple handles billing directly; we don't have access to cancel or refund it from our side, but Apple's own refund request process is available through the same Subscriptions screen.

Is my habit data private?
Yes — HabitGorilla is local-first. Your habits and history stay on your device; there's no account and no HabitGorilla server storing them. See the full Privacy Policy for exactly what does and doesn't leave your device (for example, when you use AI habit creation).

How do I delete my data?
Delete individual habits in-app, or uninstall HabitGorilla to remove everything at once — there's no separate account to close.

Something's not working — what should I include?
Your iOS version, iPhone model if you know it, and whether it happens every time or occasionally. A screenshot helps a lot if it's a visual issue.
